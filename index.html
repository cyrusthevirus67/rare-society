FAQ

Questions.

Something else? Email cyrus@rareft.com.

How do I get access after I join?

Right after checkout, go to the member login and sign in with the email you used to pay. We email you a one-time sign-in link — no password — and every lesson is unlocked.

What’s the difference between the membership and the course?

The course is the recorded lessons, yours to keep for a one-time price. The membership includes the same course plus the monthly live Q&A, the private community, and every new lesson we add — for as long as you’re a member.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, anytime. Request it from your account page or email us, and it won’t renew. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

When is the live Q&A?

Once a month. The date, time, and join link are posted in your member area ahead of each session.

Do I need a big following?

No. The course starts from the beginning and works whether you have zero followers or you’re already established.

Which platforms does it cover?

The lessons focus on short-form video — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook — and the same ideas carry over to LinkedIn and X.

Is this for businesses or creators?

Both. Business owners learn how to turn social media into customers; creators learn how to grow an audience and turn it into income.

Do I need expensive equipment?

No. Everything in the course can be filmed and edited on your phone.
